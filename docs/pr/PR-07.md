# PR07: measured outcomes and confidence feedback

Lesson: Evaluate observations before using them as memory.

Evaluate latest reading compares a newer MySolArk scrape with its matching forecast interval.
Metrics are point-power errors in kW, not measured interval energy. Raw observations, metrics,
and confidence summaries have separate tables and lineage. Repeating the same observation for one publication
cannot increase its comparison count. The evaluation runs through a bounded, checkpointed graph.

The sunny fixture has an explicitly synthetic cloudy outcome. Its solar overestimate lowers
future confidence from reduced to low; power intervals and the voltage policy stay unchanged.
Real and synthetic feedback stay separate. A 0.25 kW mean-overestimate threshold is a demo
confidence signal, not a calibrated probability or forecast correction.

Verification: `make verify` passed (25 tests + 7 Playwright checks); includes deduplication, unchanged power/policy, and the
Playwright forecast → evaluate → forecast loop. A real-source Playwright evaluation measured
0.410 kW solar overestimation and 0.076 kW availability error against a newer MySolArk scrape. Migration risk LOW: three additive tables.
Retain feedback tables for rollback. No human testing is claimed.
